# 06 · 练习（中译英）

### A. 场景句

1. 我晚几分钟到，你们先开始吧！
<details><summary>参考答案</summary>

*I'm running a few minutes late — please get started without me!*
</details>

2. 有人知道服务器是不是挂了吗？还是只有我这样？
<details><summary>参考答案</summary>

*Is the server acting weird for anyone? Or is it just me?*
</details>

3. （私信）嗨，我是 XX 组的博士生。能不能和你换一下周三的汇报时间？太麻烦的话完全没关系。
<details><summary>参考答案</summary>

*Hey! I'm a PhD student in XX's group. Is there a chance we could swap our Wednesday slots? Totally okay if that doesn't work — no pressure!*
</details>

4. 抱歉没早点给你反馈！我得先处理几篇快截止的稿子。
<details><summary>参考答案</summary>

*Sorry I didn't get to your feedback earlier! I had to prioritize a few submissions with closer deadlines.*
</details>

5. 没事，完全理解。你能回我邮件我就已经很开心了！
<details><summary>参考答案</summary>

*No worries, I totally understand. I was already really happy you replied to my email!*
</details>

6. 我们几个周五打算去食堂吃午饭，11:30 在厨房集合一起走过去，欢迎加入！
<details><summary>参考答案</summary>

*Some of us are thinking about grabbing lunch at the dining hall on Friday! We'll meet by the kitchen at 11:30 and walk over. Feel free to join!*
</details>

7. 我不太喜欢标题的前半部分，不过这很主观。
<details><summary>参考答案</summary>

*I'm not crazy about the first half of the title, but it's very subjective / personal preference 🙂*
</details>

8. 可能是个幼稚的问题，但这个方向两边都可能有意义，不是吗？
<details><summary>参考答案</summary>

*Maybe this is a naive question, but it's possible for this direction to be meaningful both ways, no?*
</details>

9. 一直想把这个搭起来，但还没顾上。
<details><summary>参考答案</summary>

*I've been meaning to set it up, but haven't gotten to it yet.*
</details>

10. 恭喜进入面试！
<details><summary>参考答案</summary>

*Congrats on making it to the interview!*
</details>

### B. 选词填空

用这些词填空：`slack` · `float` · `flesh out` · `ghost` · `temperature check` · `return the favor` · `ping`

1. Can I get a quick ______ on which tools people actually pay for?
2. I'd rather explain the issue than ______ them.
3. They're new to this, so let's cut them some ______.
4. Happy to ______ anytime!
5. I just wanted to ______ the idea and see what people think.
6. It's fine if you're still trying to ______ the idea.
7. ______ me if you need access.

<details><summary>答案</summary>

1. temperature check　2. ghost　3. slack　4. return the favor　5. float　6. flesh out　7. Ping
</details>
